# Technology Products Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview
This project analyses a technology product catalogue using Microsoft Power BI. It explores product pricing, brand representation, device categories, and countries of origin through an interactive dashboard.

## Objectives
- Analyse the number of products and distinct brands.
- Compare average product prices across brands.
- Identify the lowest- and highest-priced products.
- Explore device types and countries of origin.
- Present findings through interactive visualisations.

## Tools and Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Power Query for data cleaning and transformation
- DAX for measures and calculations
- Data visualisation and exploratory analysis

## Data Preparation
The dataset was cleaned and prepared in Power Query before being loaded into Power BI. Data types were checked, and the report was validated to ensure that its measures and visuals displayed correctly.

## Key Performance Indicators
- **Total Products:** 30
- **Distinct Brands:** 10
- **Lowest Price:** US$229
- **Highest Price:** US$2,000
- **Average Price:** Approximately US$1,060

## Key Findings
- The catalogue contains 30 products across 10 brands.
- Product prices range from US$229 to US$2,000.
- Apple has the highest average product price in the analysed dataset.
- Galaxy is the most common device label.
- The United States is the most represented country of origin.

## Interactive Dashboard
The report includes KPI cards, a brand average-price chart, a product detail table, and slicers for Brand, Device, and Country of Origin.

A separate Findings & Business Insights page summarises the main observations and recommendations.

## Business Recommendations
- Compare average prices across brands to understand differences in product positioning.
- Use the interactive filters to investigate specific device and country groups.
- Expand the catalogue to include additional products and attributes.
- Combine catalogue data with sales and cost data for future revenue and profitability analysis.

## Limitations
This dataset describes a product catalogue rather than sales transactions. It cannot independently establish revenue, profitability, customer demand, or market share.
